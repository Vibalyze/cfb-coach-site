Privacy policy

Signee: College Football Coach for iOS and Android · Last updated 31 October 2026

The game does not collect any data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no servers and no code that sends anything anywhere. Your career is saved on your own phone, and that is the only place it exists.

Who we are

The game is made and published by Vibalyze OÜ. Because the app collects no personal data, we are not the controller of any personal data through it. You can reach us at vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics service, no advertising libraries, no crash reporting service, no in-app purchase libraries and no social or sign-in integrations. It does not ask for your email address, your contacts, your location, your photos, your microphone or your camera. It does not read your advertising identifier or any other device identifier.

The app works fully offline. On Android its manifest does not request the INTERNET permission. The only system features it uses are the one that tells it where its own private storage folder is, so it can write your save there, and the system’s own “rate this app” prompt, described below.

What the app stores on your device

Your career — your coach, the fictional schools, rosters, schedule, results, polls and recruiting board — is saved as one compressed JSON file, dynasty.json.gz, inside the app’s own documents folder. The game also keeps a copy of the previous save next to it (dynasty.json.gz.bak) so a damaged file can be recovered.

The save contains game state only: no device information, no identifiers and nothing about you. None of it is transmitted.

A second, tiny file, review.json, records only when the game last offered the rating prompt, so that it does not ask too often. It holds dates and counts and nothing about you.

All of the game’s content ships inside the app. Nothing is downloaded at runtime, so there is no request to a content server and nothing to log.

Device backups

Because the save lives in the app’s documents folder, it may be included in your phone’s normal backup (Android Auto Backup, or device or iCloud backup on iOS), depending on your settings. Those backups are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies. We cannot see them, and we have no way to read your save.

The rating prompt

After a big moment, such as a first bowl win, the game may ask the operating system to show its standard “rate this app” sheet. That sheet is drawn and run by Apple or Google, not by us, and they decide whether it actually appears. If you leave a rating or review, it goes to the store under the store’s own privacy policy, and we only ever see what the store publishes.

Accounts, online play, leaderboards

There are none. There is no login, no friends list, no chat, no cloud save and no online leaderboard. Every other coach, player and recruit in the game is invented by the game. The entire game works with the phone in aeroplane mode.

Advertising and purchases

Version 1.0 contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. There is no ad network in the build, so there is no ad profiling, no App Tracking Transparency prompt and nothing to opt out of.

A later version may add a one-time “Pro” purchase and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the store privacy answers will be updated to match.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its documents folder, and with it your save. There is nothing left on a server, because there is no server.

Two things to know:

  • Your phone’s backup may still hold a copy of the save after you delete the app. Clearing that is done in your device’s backup settings, not in the game.
  • Starting a new career overwrites the existing one. There is one save at a time.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age: there are no ads, no purchases, no chat, no user profiles and no way for one player to see another player’s content. Nothing a child does in the game leaves the device.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information at all. If you believe something in the app contradicts this, please write to the address below and we will fix it.

Your rights

Data protection law, including the GDPR, gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. Your save is yours, on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this page does not describe — analytics, ads, purchases, cloud save — this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, and the store privacy forms are updated with it. We will not quietly add collection under an old policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything on this page: vibalyze@zohomail.eu.
