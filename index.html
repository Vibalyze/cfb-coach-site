Signee: College Football Coach

Take over a struggling small program. Make the calls on Saturday. Build a dynasty.

  • You make the calls Every game stops for the big moments: fourth down, go for two, the onside kick. Each button shows its odds, and they are the true odds.
  • Recruit your class Scout, call, visit and offer. A few taps a week, then signing day shows who you landed.
  • Climb the ladder Beat expectations and bigger schools come calling. Miss them and the hot seat warms up.

All fictional

Every school, conference, bowl, coach and player in the game is invented. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any college, university, conference, league or association.

What it does not do

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no internet connection needed. Version 1.0 has no ads and no in-app purchases. Your career is saved on your own phone and nowhere else. See the privacy policy.
